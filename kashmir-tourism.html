Why Visit Kashmir?

Often called Paradise on Earth, Kashmir offers a unique blend of natural beauty and cultural charm. From the iconic Dal Lake houseboats to the meadows of Gulmarg and Pahalgam, every destination feels postcard-perfect.

Srinagar

Dal Lake | Mughal Gardens | Shikara Rides

Enjoy peaceful lake cruises, floating markets, and traditional Kashmiri cuisine.

Gulmarg

Skiing | Gondola | Alpine Views

Ideal for winter sports and panoramic mountain scenery throughout the year.

Pahalgam

Lidder Valley | Riverside Stays | Trekking

Perfect for nature lovers seeking quiet valleys, pine forests, and light adventure.

Sonamarg

Glaciers | Scenic Drives | Meadow Trails

A beautiful base for mountain excursions and breathtaking photo opportunities.

Suggested 4-Day Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Arrive in Srinagar, evening shikara ride on Dal Lake.
  2. Day 2: Day trip to Gulmarg, enjoy gondola and local sightseeing.
  3. Day 3: Visit Pahalgam valley and explore riverside spots.
  4. Day 4: Sonamarg excursion and departure from Srinagar.

Plan Your Kashmir Holiday

Best seasons: March to May for spring blooms, and December to February for snowfall.
